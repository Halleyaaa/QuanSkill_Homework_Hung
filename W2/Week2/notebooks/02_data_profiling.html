Interpretation. Among the 229 ticket questions with a valid timestamp, 25 have no label (10.9%). The gap is not uniform: on Mondays the missing rate is 30.0% (9/30), compared with 8.0% (16/199) on other days. Monday is the highest-load day, so the most plausible mechanism is MAR (missing at random given queue load): agents skip the optional label when they are busy. The evidence does not prove the mechanism. Saturday is also high (6/27), and the samples are small. Practical consequences: (1) dropping unlabelled questions would under-represent Monday traffic in the evaluation set; (2) imputing labels with the most frequent intent would inject wrong ground truth. The pipeline therefore sends these questions to review for human labelling. It does not delete them and does not guess labels.
